CONTROLS

Movement: A/D or Left/Right Arrow Keys
Jump: Space / W / Up Arrow
Restart Level: R
Next Level: N
Exit Level: ESC

HOW TO PLAY

Navigate through 12 challenging levels filled with deceptive platforms and deadly traps. Every platform might be a trap, every safe-looking path could lead to disaster. Use the restart feature wisely and learn from your mistakes.
