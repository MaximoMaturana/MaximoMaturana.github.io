GAME FEATURES

Detailed breakdown of Trust Issues mechanics and gameplay elements.

PLATFORM TYPES

Trust Issues features a variety of deceptive platform types designed to challenge player expectations:

  • Fake Platforms: Appear solid but disappear when stepped on
  • Falling Platforms: Collapse after a short delay
  • Moving Platforms: Shift horizontally or vertically
  • Invisible Platforms: Only visible when close or under certain conditions
  • Conveyor Belts: Push the player in unexpected directions
  • Bounce Pads: Launch the player with varying force

TRAP MECHANICS

The game includes multiple spike trap behaviors that add layers of complexity:

  • Static Spikes: Always visible and deadly
  • Moving Spikes: Patrol platforms or walls
  • Hidden Spikes: Appear when triggered
  • Control Inversion Zones: Reverse player controls temporarily

GAME MODES

Trust Issues offers different gameplay experiences:

  • Normal Mode: Standard gameplay through 12 levels
  • Mirrored Mode: Controls are flipped for extra challenge
  • Level Restart: Quick restart on player input (R key)

TECHNICAL DETAILS

Built with Python 3.10+ and Pygame library, featuring custom collision detection, sprite management, and level loading systems.
