Follice Box

A conceptual autonomous follow-me cooler box — all-terrain motorized chassis, 50 L insulated storage, differential steering, and full engineering calculations targeting a €300–400 budget.

Overview

What does it do?

Follice Box is a conceptual autonomous cooler platform designed to follow a user across different outdoor terrains — beaches, parks, campsites — without needing to be pushed or carried. It combines a motorized all-terrain chassis with a 50 L insulated cooler box, controlled through differential steering with speed reduction.

The project covers the full engineering design process: requirements definition, concept sketches, 3D CAD modeling, engineering calculations, component selection, power distribution, chassis and cooler assembly, and a complete bill of materials — all targeting a €300–400 budget with standard DIN components.

Key Specs

  • ⚖️ Design mass: ~60 kg
  • 📦 Cooler capacity: 50 L
  • 🏃 Max speed: 1.2 m/s
  • ⛰️ Slope capability: 10°
  • 🔋 Runtime: ~3 hours
  • ⚡ Voltage: 24 V system
  • 📏 Chassis: 863 × 500 × 156 mm
  • 💶 Budget target: €300–400

Project Scope

How the design was developed

Design Highlights

Key engineering decisions

⚙️

All-Terrain Chassis

Four motorized wheels with 35 mm ground clearance. Differential steering achieved by reducing one rear wheel's speed — no dedicated steering motor required.

🧊

50 L Cooler Box

External dims 600 × 400 × 350 mm with 30 mm HDPE walls and PU foam insulation. Brass and aluminium closing mechanisms for durability.

🔋

24 V Power System

Full power distribution flow chart maps the electrical architecture — from battery to motor drivers and auxiliary systems — targeting ~3 hours of runtime.

📋

DIN-Standard BOM

All mechanical components selected from standard DIN catalogues, keeping costs within the €300–400 target and simplifying sourcing and future iteration.
