MISLEADING MECHANICS

Navigate through 12 levels where nothing is as it seems. Platforms disappear, controls invert, and traps lurk in unexpected places. Every level is designed to subvert your expectations and challenge your platforming instincts.

PLATFORM VARIETY

Experience diverse platform behaviors: fake platforms that vanish, falling blocks, moving platforms, invisible traps, conveyor belts, and bounce pads. Each platform type adds layers of complexity and surprise.

TRAP SYSTEMS

Multiple spike trap behaviors create tension: static spikes, moving patrols, hidden threats, and control inversion zones. Master the art of mistrust as you progress through increasingly deceptive challenges.

KEY FEATURES

Designed and implemented using Python and Pygame.

12 LEVELS

Progressive difficulty with unique level designs, each introducing new platform types and trap combinations.

PLATFORM VARIETY

Fake platforms, falling blocks, moving platforms, invisible traps, conveyor belts, and bounce pads.

TRAP SYSTEMS

Static spikes, moving patrols, hidden threats, and control inversion zones for maximum tension.

QUICK RESTART

Press R to instantly restart any level, encouraging experimentation and learning from mistakes.

MIRRORED MODE

Extra challenge mode with flipped controls for replayability and increased difficulty.

SIMPLE CONTROLS

A/D or Arrow Keys for movement, Space/W for jump, R to restart, ESC to exit level.
